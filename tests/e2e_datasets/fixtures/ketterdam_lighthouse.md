# Ketterdam Point lighthouse — keeper's log extract

## Lamp replacement, 1965

The Ketterdam Point lighthouse lamp was replaced on 17 April 1965 by keeper
Hestia Marlow, who logged a visibility of 14 nautical miles on the first night
of operation. The replaced lamp had been in service since the station was
automated.

## Fog signal

The fog signal was a diaphone sounding two blasts every ninety seconds. Keeper
Marlow recorded that the diaphone was audible from the Quarry Line embankment
in a northerly wind.

## Decommissioning

The station was decommissioned in 1998. The optic is held at the Ketterdam
maritime collection, catalogue reference KPL-1965-04.
